the thesis

A linker already computes, for every byte, its final address and value. A packer then re-derives those facts. wupz keeps them as a first-class layout plan and packs during layout — one pass, no temp file.

QuantTern — the emotional ternary code

Move the three axes. Each is quantized to a trit at |x| ≥ 0.33; a value nearer zero is a real don't know, not a small yes. Four trits pack to a byte; the tag is the fingerprint — shared across wupz, qwave, and alexiai (gaia-mlx-quant::wupz).
